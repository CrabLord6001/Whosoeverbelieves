Days 1–7

Week 1 - Rooted in God’s Word

“As therefore you received Christ Jesus the Lord, walk in him, rooted and built up in him and established in the faith, even as you were taught, abounding in it in thanksgiving.”

Colossians 2:6–7
Notes

Try to be consistent for the sake of building a natural routine.

For your memory verse, summarize the meaning in your own words from the context and key words (see the “The Hermeneutical Spiral”) and try to diagram the sentences (this grammarly article is a helpful start). Think of mnemonics and personal connections to each block of the verse (see “The Power to Memorize Scripture”).

  • Make a Bible reading plan for the month
  • Pick a time of day to read and stick to it at least 5 days this week
  • Choose a verse to memorize (you’ll add a new one each week)
  • Read the chapter around your memory verse and write a short paragraph summarizing what it meant to the original readers, with a few applications to your life
  • Block diagramming: Break your memory verse into the logical clauses and color or underline the verbs
  • Say your verse from memory by the end of the week
  • Jot down questions as you read, and take at least one to an elder or mature believing friend
  • Pick a friend to check in with each week, and share a verse or passage with them

Days 8–14

Week 2 - Drawing Near with Prayer

“Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners. Purify your hearts, you double-minded.”

James 4:8
Notes

Having a fixed prayer hour at the end of the day gives you time to reflect back in gratitude. It also forces you to remove yourself from screentime and rest easier. Or pray in the morning to set the tone for the day. Pick what works for you!

Prayer should be intentional to intercede for others.

Still goingDaily readingMemory verseFriend check-in
  • Pray before you read, then pray the passage back to God after
  • Set a daily prayer time, morning or night
  • Pray through ACTS (Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, Supplications) at least 5 days
  • Ask 3 people what you can pray for, and check back in with each of them
  • Make a calming worship playlist for your prayer time
  • Write down 3–5 things you’re thankful for at least 5 days this week

Days 15–21

Week 3 - Quiet the Noise

“Be still, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations. I will be exalted in the earth.”

Psalm 46:10
Notes

If you need social media for work, consider the level of separation that would be practical (deleting the app, using an alt, setting away messages, etc…)

If you’re used to using a Bible app, this is a good opportunity to focus on traditional journaling and note taking in a physical Bible.

Still goingDaily readingMemory versePrayer timeGratitude list
  • Pick 1–2 things to fast from this week, like social media, video games, or junk food
  • Keep the fast all week (slipped? Just pick it back up)
  • Take notes by hand in a physical Bible or journal at least 5 days
  • Set aside one rest day this week
