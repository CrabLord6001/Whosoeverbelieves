The Herodian Dynasty

Five men in this family are called Herod in the New Testament, and three of its women appear by name.

Notes

Herodias's first husband

Mark and Matthew call Herodias's first husband Philip, and the chart follows them. Josephus names him Herod, the son of Herod the Great by Mariamne II, and says Herodias bore him Salome (Antiquities 18.136). Christian apologists generally reconcile this issue in two ways. The first takes Philip as a second name, on the pattern of Herod Antipas and Herod Agrippa, who each carried Herod along with another name; on that reading the Gospels and Josephus describe the same man. The second takes the Gospels to mean Philip the Tetrarch, who later married Herodias's daughter. The chart shows the Gospel name and leaves the identification to this note.

Dates

Herod died in 4 BC, resting on Josephus and on the lunar eclipse he mentions before it. The chart uses that date throughout, and the dates for Archelaus, Antipas and Philip follow from it.

Sources

Josephus, Antiquities of the Jews 17.19–22 and 18.109–137, and War of the Jews 1.552–563, for the wives, the children and the marriages. The New Testament references are given in full.
