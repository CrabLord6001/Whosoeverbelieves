Act 6, the Age of the Church, closes with the Church taken to heaven, the Tribulation on earth, and Christ’s return with His Church. Act 7 is the Millennial Kingdom. The epilogue is the new heaven, the new earth, and the New Jerusalem.

The Key to Revelation

Ch. 1“the things which you have seen”
Ch. 2–3“the things which are”
Ch. 4–22“the things which will happen hereafter”

John’s own outline (Revelation 1:19): the vision of Christ, the letters to the seven churches, and the events still to come.

Two Different Judgments

The believer will never be judged to decide whether he has eternal life (John 5:24). The Judgment Seat of Christ decides rewards. The Great White Throne is for those who did not believe.

Notes & Sources

  1. This chart follows the pretribulational, premillennial view taught by Philippe R. Sterling and the Grace Evangelical Society.
  2. The Rapture and the Second Coming are two events: Christ comes for His Church in the air (1 Thessalonians 4:16–17) and later returns with His saints to the earth (Revelation 19:14; Zechariah 14:4–5).
  3. The seven years are Daniel’s seventieth week (Daniel 9:27). See Daniel’s Seventy Weeks.
  4. Satan is bound during the thousand years and released afterward for a final rebellion (Revelation 20:1–3; Revelation 20:7–10).
