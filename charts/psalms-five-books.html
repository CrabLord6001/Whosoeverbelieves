Who Wrote the Psalms?

Counts come from the titles (superscriptions) at the head of each psalm. Psalm 88 names both the sons of Korah and Heman, so it is counted under each.

The Messianic Psalms

Notes & Sources

  1. Author counts are taken from the psalm titles as printed in the World English Bible. The titles are part of the Hebrew text.
  2. Book II ends with “This ends the prayers by David, the son of Jesse” (Psalm 72:20), though more psalms by David appear later. The note likely marks the close of an earlier collection.
  3. Jewish tradition compares the five books of Psalms to the five books of Moses (Midrash Tehillim on Psalm 1).
  4. Psalms 42–83 are often called the “Elohistic Psalter” because they mostly use the name God (Elohim) where the rest of the Psalter uses Yahweh.
