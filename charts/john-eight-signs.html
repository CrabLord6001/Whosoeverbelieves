The Purpose

Written So That You May Believe

The Big Idea

The Gospel of John presents eight signs Jesus performed so that anyone may believe His promise of eternal life and thus have everlasting life in His name. In essence John’s message is faith alone in Christ alone for everlasting life.

Philippe R. Sterling

The Eight Signs

John organizes his Gospel around eight signs that bear witness that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God. The eighth, His own death and resurrection, is the sign that all the others point to.

Following Jesus Through John

A route diagram, not a scale map. John follows Jesus back and forth between Galilee and Jerusalem, and many scenes are set at a feast. The three Passovers (John 2:13; John 6:4; John 11:55) are the main reason His public ministry is dated at about three years.

Notes & Sources

  1. The count of eight signs, with the death and resurrection as the eighth, follows Philippe R. Sterling. Many outlines count seven signs before the cross.
  2. The feast in John 5:1 is not named. It may have been a Passover, which would add a year to the ministry.
  3. The Greek verb pisteuō (“believe”) appears 98 times in John, more than in any other New Testament book.
  4. John never uses the noun “faith.” He always uses the verb: believing in Jesus for everlasting life (John 3:16; John 5:24; John 6:47).
