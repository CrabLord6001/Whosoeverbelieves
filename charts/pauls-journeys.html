Seven Progress Reports in Acts

Luke follows the outline of Acts 1:8: Jerusalem, then Judea and Samaria, then the ends of the earth. He closes each stage with a short progress report on the spread of the Word.

  1. 1:1–2:47Acts
    Jerusalem

    The Church is established in Jerusalem

    Progress report: Acts 2:47
  2. 2:48–6:7Acts
    Jerusalem

    The Church is extended in Jerusalem

    Progress report: Acts 6:7
  3. 6:8–9:31Acts
    Judea & Samaria

    The Church scatters

    Progress report: Acts 9:31
  4. 9:32–12:24Acts
    Syrian Antioch

    The Church is extended to Syrian Antioch

    Progress report: Acts 12:24
  5. 12:25–16:5Acts
    Asia Minor

    The Church is extended to Asia Minor

    Progress report: Acts 16:5
  6. 16:6–19:20Acts
    The Aegean

    The Church is extended to the Aegean area

    Progress report: Acts 19:20
  7. 19:21–28:31Acts
    Rome

    The Church is extended to the ends of the earth

    Progress report: Acts 28:30–31

Notes & Sources

  1. Town locations are plotted from their known ancient sites. The lines between stops are straight and only suggest the roads and sea lanes Paul used.
  2. On the second and third journeys Acts names regions (“Syria and Cilicia,” “the region of Galatia and Phrygia”) rather than every town (Acts 15:41; Acts 18:23). The map routes those legs through the churches founded on the first journey.
  3. Dates follow the same chronology as When Paul Wrote His Letters.
