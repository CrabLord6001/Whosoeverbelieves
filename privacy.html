Privacy

What this site records, and why

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Whosoever Believes is a personal ministry site. This page explains, in plain terms, what the site records about you and why. The short version: you can read everything here without an account, and if you never sign in, the site never learns who you are.

Reading Without an Account

You don't need an account to read anything on this site.

When you visit a page, the site records the page address, the site that linked you here (if any), whether your screen is phone-sized, and the time. It does not record your IP address, does not set a cookie for this, and has no way to connect one visit to another — a short random label is held in your browser's memory for the length of the tab and then discarded. These numbers exist so I can see which articles are being read. They cannot identify you.

If you use the search box, the words you search for are recorded, along with how many results came back. Searches that return nothing are the most useful thing on that list: they tell me what readers came looking for that I haven't written yet.

This site also uses Google Analytics, which does set cookies and does receive your IP address. That is Google's processing, governed by Google's privacy policy, and your browser's tracking protections or an ad blocker will stop it.

Page fonts are served by Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Both receive your request, and therefore your IP address, as any web host does.

If You Make an Account

Accounts are optional and exist only to save your own work. When you sign up — by email or with Google — the site stores your email address and whatever display name you choose.

Depending on what you use, your account may also hold: your progress through a Bible reading plan and any reflections you write; your discipleship challenge progress; your progress in the Greek study tools; and, in Bible Memory, the verses you are memorizing, how you have broken them into phrases, the memory rooms you placed them in, the notes you wrote for each phrase, and your review scores.

All of that is yours. It is visible only to you, enforced at the database level, and it exists so the site can give it back to you next time.

Comments

If you leave a comment, the site stores what you wrote and the account that wrote it. Comments are reviewed before they appear, so nothing you write is public until I have approved it. Approved comments are visible to anyone reading that article.

Notes I May Keep

I can attach a private note to a reader's account — for instance to remember a conversation we have had, or that someone has been abusive and shouldn't be approved again. You cannot see these notes, and neither can anyone else; they are readable only by the site's own administrative function. If you want to know whether I hold a note about you, or want it deleted, ask and I will tell you.

Email

If you turn on article updates, your email address is shared with Resend, the service that actually sends the mail, so that it can deliver to you. You are never added to this list without asking for it, and every message carries an unsubscribe link. You can also turn updates off in your account settings at any time.

I do not sell anything, and I do not share your email with anyone other than the service that delivers it.

Where This Data Lives

Accounts, comments, reading plans and the rest are stored with Supabase, which hosts the database on my behalf. Email delivery is handled by Resend. These are processors: they hold the data to provide the service, not for their own purposes.

Children

This site is not aimed at children, and accounts are not intended for anyone under 13. If you believe a child has made an account here, tell me and I will remove it.

What You Can Ask For

Write to me at CONTACT-ADDRESS-GOES-HERE and I will:

  • tell you what the site holds about you,
  • send you a copy of it,
  • correct anything that's wrong,
  • delete your account and everything attached to it.

Deleting your account removes your profile, your reading plan progress, your reflections, your memorization work, and your comments. I will do this on request and without asking why.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves. Nothing here is retroactive — I will not start doing something new with data already collected without saying so first.
